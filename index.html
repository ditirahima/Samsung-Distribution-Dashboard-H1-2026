INSIGHT 01 — SAFETY STOCK
47.2%
APAC Carries Disproportionate Demand Risk
APAC represents 47.2% of global revenue ($87.3bn) and volume but operates under 'Tight' capacity with 33% Medium supplier risk. Modelled safety stock requirement: ~19,487 units/000 — highest of any region. A demand surge ≥15% creates stockout risk within 5 days without DC capacity expansion in China and India. Buffer currently 21.6d vs 25d target.
📊 Key Metrics
SS req: ~19,487 units/000 · Buffer 21.6d vs 25d target · 15 DC branches
INSIGHT 02 — LEAD TIME VARIANCE
σ 2.185d
Display & Harman: 2.185× Lead Time Variance vs Peers
Display Panels and Harman/Auto Electronics exhibit σ=2.185 days versus 0.000 for Memory and 0.826 for Foundry — 165% higher. OLED panel fragility and automotive multi-market customs certification drive this variance. Combined delay pool: 14,975 units/000 per period. Deferred revenue risk: ~$163mm per H1.
⚠ Risk Signals
Max lead: 14d (2.3× avg) · Delay pool: 14,975 units/000 · $163mm risk
INSIGHT 03 — CROSS-DOCK EFFICIENCY
0.65×
Distributor/Retail Channel: 65% Cross-Dock Factor — Network Best
The Distributor/Retail channel achieves 6.5d avg lead vs 8.5d Carrier/Operator — cross-dock efficiency factor 0.65, best in network. With 444,395 units/000 H1 volume and 21.9d buffer, this is the prime DC pre-positioning candidate. Shifting 10% of OEM volume to cross-dock routing reduces blended lead by ~0.4d and saves ~$38mm annually.

Shift 10% OEM Volume to Cross-Dock Routing
